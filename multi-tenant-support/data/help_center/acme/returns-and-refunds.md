# Returns and refunds

You can return any Acme Robotics product within 30 days of delivery for a full refund.
The product does not need to be unopened, but it must include the base station and the power cable.

To start a return, open a support ticket with your order number. We email you a prepaid DHL label within one business day.
Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 business days after the parcel reaches our warehouse.

Spare parts such as brushes and filters can be returned unopened within 30 days.
