# Battery life and short cleaning runs

A fully charged Sweepr S2 cleans for about 120 minutes on the Standard power setting and about 60 minutes on Max.

If the vacuum stops after a few minutes, clean the brush roll and the wheel axles first: tangled hair makes the motor draw more power and the vacuum returns to the base early.
A battery that holds less than 30 minutes of charge after cleaning is covered by the 2-year warranty.
